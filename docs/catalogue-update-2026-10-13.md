## Protection against drift

Weights remain 25/15/5/5/15/15/20. Length remains off by default, capped at +0.5% if enabled. No ideology, nationality, genre, popularity or acclaim adjustment was added.

Released episodes and assessed episodes are separate facts. Fallout lists sixteen releases while its score still covers season one's eight. Andor lists twenty-four releases while retaining its legacy twelve-episode input and an unresolved score cutoff. The detail panel exposes these gaps; a count update cannot substitute for a new assessment.

“Recently added” exposes the intake without treating pending shows as zeroes. Full cards, ranks under posters and coloured bars are preserved. New assessments have no invented historical archive. Personal ratings and accounts remain separate.

The [maintenance manifest](../data/maintenance/2026-10-13.json) lists every addition, exact new input vector, release check, discovery source and deferred candidate. A digest protects the unchanged original records. [CATALOGUE-MAINTENANCE.md](CATALOGUE-MAINTENANCE.md) and [ASSESSMENTS.md](ASSESSMENTS.md) govern repeat visits. The superseded directive now points to these rules instead of obsolete weights, quotas and penalties.
