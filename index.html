Length adjustment

This optional legacy rule favours longer runs. It is a preference, not a measure of quality. It can take a final score above 10; turning it off uses ×1.00 for every show.

What counts as an assessment?

Ratings, reasons and episode counts must refer to the same run. A selected-season entry excludes later material. Where the original season cutoff was not recorded, we say so.

Starting ratings are subjective, AI-assisted editorial judgments. New research assessments identify their scope, sources and evidence limits; they are provisional interpretations, not claims of complete viewing. Historical notes are labelled separately from current ratings; a matching number alone does not verify a review. Known wrong-programme assessments stay searchable and can be shortlisted, but receive no score or rank until reassessed.

Ongoing stories are judged by the promises developed within the assessed run. A future ending is unknown, not automatically bad. Purposeful quiet scenes, grounded worlds and coherent ambiguity can all earn high ratings.

A note on the project

A starting point for your taste.

Dynamic Rank Engine is an independent television ranking project. The catalogue and original AI-assisted reviews are an editorial starting point. Your formula and ratings determine your personal order.

Original reviews may contain spoilers, older scores and outdated streaming availability. Archived notes are historical evidence. New research assessments are labelled separately and disclose their sources and limitations. Known premise errors are withheld, and unresolved factual concerns are identified. We do not claim daily updates or live availability.

Privacy & storage

Guest preferences stay in this browser’s local storage. Signing in uses Google Firebase Authentication; ratings, weights, shortlist and filters are saved to the existing Dynamic Rank Engine Firebase project. An account-specific recovery copy is also kept on this device. Signing out returns to your separate guest rankings.

Posters load from The Movie Database, and sign-in loads scripts from Google. Those services receive the ordinary network information needed to serve requests. The site does not include advertising or an analytics tracker.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
